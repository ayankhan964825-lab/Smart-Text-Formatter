QUESTIONS

Small print, no surprises.

Is it really free? +

Yes — the tool is fully free and open-source. You just bring your own Gemini API key (Google gives one free at aistudio.google.com).

Where is my data stored? +

Nowhere on our servers. Documents live in your browser (IndexedDB). The AI request goes directly to Google using your key.

Can I export to Word without formatting loss? +

Yes. The DOCX exporter preserves paragraph styles, headings, page breaks and image placement. PDF uses the browser's native print engine for pixel-perfect output.

What about tables, math and diagrams? +

Tables render natively. Equations use KaTeX. Mermaid diagrams render as vectors and export cleanly to all formats.

Is there an API? +

Not yet — but the whole engine is open on GitHub. Star the repo if you'd like it prioritized.
