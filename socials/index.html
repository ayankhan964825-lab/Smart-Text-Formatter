The Science of Well-being

Understanding PERMA

PERMA is a scientifically-backed model developed by Dr. Martin Seligman, the father of Positive Psychology, introduced in 2011.

It provides a comprehensive framework for flourishing and authentic happiness — going beyond mere pleasure to encompass meaning, engagement, and achievement.

"Well-being is a combination of feeling good and functioning effectively." — Martin Seligman
